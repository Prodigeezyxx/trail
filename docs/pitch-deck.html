WHAT IT IS

This is not a news feed.
It is a workspace.

1. Find a fact

Browse by track, country or layer. Every record names its source, when it was last checked, and what it does not tell you.

2. Follow the trail

Each record names a holder, a witness and the exact question to ask. A next step, not just a fact.

3. Leave with a packet

Send, print, broadcast or carry. Four formats for four real channels. The packet is the product.

THREE TRACKS

Every record belongs to one.

Transparency & Accountability

Turn a published budget, tender, audit finding or service standard into a written question with a named recipient and a response date.

Stability & Social Cohesion

Route everyday friction — a broken water point, a land question, a shared-resource dispute — to the body that can resolve it, before it escalates.

Safety, Reporting & Protection

Write a report that carries no identifiers, get a reference, and reach a real pathway: oversight body, legal aid, or survivor support.

TOP-DOWN: TRAILS

14 seeded records, 13 countries.

Each record carries:

  • Source name, type, URL, update cadence
  • Last-checked date (not assumed current)
  • Explicit limitations (required field)
  • Separate confidence & importance scores
  • Named holder + witness
  • The exact question to ask

EXAMPLE: MAKUENI, KENYA

Holder: County water services office

Witness: Community water committee

Ask: “Who is responsible for this water point, and when can the community expect a maintenance update?”

GRADUATION

  • ① Resident raises an issue with no votes
  • ② At 3 votes → community-verified
  • ③ At 5 votes → promoted to official trail with holder + date

Votes are local. Without a relay, nobody else sees them. Every issue card says so.

PROVENANCE RULES

Five rules, never broken.

①A source is never shown as verified unless it is. 0 of 14 seeded records are independently checked — stated in the UI.
②“Unchecked” is distinct from “stale”. Freshness is computed against each source's own cadence.
③Limitations are a required field. Every record states what it does not tell you.
④Confidence and importance are separate. How much it matters vs how much it can be trusted.
⑤A gap never becomes a claim. Unknown reads “unknown”. No URL reads “No URL”.

TECH & VERIFICATION

React 19, Vite, no backend.

Stack

  • React 19 + Vite
  • D3-geo (orthographic + Mercator)
  • SVG markers with icon symbols
  • Lucide icons
  • Local storage (no backend)
  • Service worker (offline cache)

Build pipeline

  • Python ingest → GeoJSON simplification
  • Vite build → dist/
  • Portable bundler → single-file HTML
  • Service worker generator
  • Playwright verification (30+ harnesses)
WHAT IS NOT BUILT

Stated so nobody has to discover it.

No server

Votes and issues cannot sync between devices.

No live data

Snapshot dated 21 Sep 2026. Nothing polls.

No encryption

Local storage is readable by anyone with device access.

No referral DB

Report routes by category, not to a verified org list.

Share buttons

Need a connection (hand text to an app).

Anonymity

No state-run mechanism confirmed to accept anonymous submissions.
